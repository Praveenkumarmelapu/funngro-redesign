Questions

Before you brief us.

How fast can a campaign go live?

After your brief, Funngro matches the cohort and most campaigns start seeing verified actions within days, with reporting delivered on a weekly rhythm.

How is work verified?

Every task requires proof - a photo, screenshot or link - reviewed before payout. That is how Funngro keeps bot traffic at zero and completion rates high.

What does pricing look like?

Pricing depends on work type, cohort and volume - cost per action for promotions and referrals, per brief for creators, per response for surveys. Funngro shares a starting point after a short note about your goals.

Is the audience really 14–25?

Yes - the pool is young India: students, creators and first-time earners. Cohorts can be targeted by age band, city and interest, and the data handling is DPDP-compliant.
