How it works

From sign-up to first payout in five steps.

No experience needed. No fees, ever. Just your phone, a UPI ID and two minutes to begin.

Download & sign up

Get Funngro from Google Play or the App Store. OTP sign-up takes two minutes - earning without investment starts here.

Pick a campaign

Browse thousands of live brand campaigns. Choose what fits you: surveys, referrals, content, promotion and more.

Complete the task

Every campaign lists clear requirements. Finish the task and upload your proof - a photo, screenshot or link.

Get verified

The brand reviews your submission. Approved work moves straight to payout - no chasing, no confusion.

Paid via UPI

Money lands in your UPI or bank account. First payout within 24 hours of verification, zero withdrawal fees.

Questions

Before you download.

Is Funngro free to join?

Yes - free forever. There is no registration fee, no deposit and no investment. You sign up with an OTP and start picking brand campaigns.

How do I get paid?

Earnings go straight to your UPI ID or bank account. Funngro states a first payout typically arrives within 24 hours of your task being verified, with zero withdrawal fees.

What do I need to start?

A smartphone or laptop with internet, a UPI ID, and two minutes to sign up. No experience or resume required.

Who can earn on Funngro?

The platform is built for young Indians aged 14–25 - students, creators and first-time earners who want flexible remote work from home.

What kind of work will I get?

Brand promotion, product sampling, referrals, influencer briefs, content creation, surveys, app testing and more, across 12+ categories from thousands of brands.
